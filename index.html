About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Dayananda Sagar University, Bengaluru (CGPA: 7.65, First Class, 2023). My academic journey covered a strong foundation in algorithms, data structures, software engineering, database systems, natural language processing, and data science.

After graduation, I joined LTIMindtree as a Package Implementation Consultant specialising in SAP Basis — working on S/4HANA migrations, system performance optimisation, and enterprise IT infrastructure. I received the Star Performer Award and multiple Super Crew Awards for my contributions.

My interest in Human Computer Interaction grew from observing how complex enterprise systems often fail users — not because of technical limitations, but due to poor interface design. I want to bridge this gap by studying HCI and building more intelligent, human-centred systems.

My final year thesis — "Detection of Thyroid Disorder using Machine Learning Approach" — reflects my interest in applying AI to solve real healthcare problems. This project strengthened my belief in building systems that are not only technically sound but also accessible and usable by non-technical users.
